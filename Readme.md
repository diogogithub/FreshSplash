# FreshSplash
More than just [bringing Microsoft Spotlight to GNU/Linux distros](https://github.com/diogogithub/microsoft-spotlight-for-gnu-linux-distros), 
I wanted that people could manage their own ~~indie Spotlight~~ FreshSplash servers and communities, that is, now it is possible
to share a FreshSplash server with your friends so that you can be surprised on your day to day with your friends' great photos
and art work.
